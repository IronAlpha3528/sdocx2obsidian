What is converted

Converted in original order

  • Typed plain text and paragraph breaks
  • Embedded images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP)
  • Voice recordings and attached audio clips
  • Standard emojis and non-Latin scripts

Skipped

  • Rich-text styling (bold, colors, highlight)
  • S-Pen handwriting, ink, and vector drawings
  • PDF and non-media document attachments
  • Canvas strokes and lasso crops (.spi)

Privacy

Privacy Statement: The page converts notes entirely in the browser; files are never uploaded; nothing is stored; closing or refreshing the tab discards everything; the code is open source.

After loading the static page, zero network requests are made. The Python converter runs locally inside your browser via WebAssembly.

Frequently asked questions

Are my notes ever uploaded to a server?

No. Every step of the conversion runs entirely inside your browser's WebAssembly environment. Your notes and media never touch any external server.

Why were my drawings or handwriting skipped?

Samsung Notes stores S-Pen ink and drawings in proprietary vector formats that are not yet reverse-engineered into standard Markdown or SVG. To preserve your note without corruption, non-image drawings are skipped.

What happens when I close or refresh the tab?

All files and in-memory caches are discarded immediately. Nothing is saved in local storage or cookies.

Which Samsung Notes files are supported?

All modern Samsung Notes exported with the .sdocx extension. Legacy .sdoc or .snb formats from older Galaxy devices are not supported.

How do I report a note that fails?

If a note fails, click "Report this problem" next to the failed note. It creates a privacy-safe GitHub issue containing only error codes and size buckets—never your note's title or contents.
