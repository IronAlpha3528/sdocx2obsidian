sdocx2obsidian

Convert Samsung Notes (.sdocx) into Obsidian Markdown vaults directly in your browser.

Mobile Device Advisory: Converting notes containing high-resolution images or long voice recordings requires significant browser memory. If you experience performance issues, pauses, or tab reloads on mobile, please continue on a laptop or desktop computer.

How it works

1
Export: Share or export notes as .sdocx from Samsung Notes.
2
Drop: Select or drop your .sdocx files into the area below.
3
Download: Download the generated ZIP and unpack directly into your Obsidian vault.
